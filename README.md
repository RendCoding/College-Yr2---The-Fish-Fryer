# 🎣 The Fish Fryer
### Year 2 Games Development — College Group Project

**The Fish Fryer** is a cosy fishing and restaurant management game based on the Eritrean coast near the Red Sea. This repository is part of my Year 2 Games Development college work and will be used to document our group project, show our progress and help build my programming portfolio.

> **Project type:** Group project

## 📖 Game Overview

The Fish Fryer is a cosy game where players fish along the Eritrean coast, collect different fish and use them to run their own restaurant.

The main idea is to combine the relaxing feeling of fishing games with the progression and customisation found in restaurant management and tycoon games.

Players can catch different fish species found in the Red Sea, such as the Eritrean butterflyfish and wahoo. Each fish can have different stats, sizes, colours and rarities, making every catch feel a bit different.

Once a fish has been caught, players can decide how to use it. They could hire it as a worker in the restaurant, use it for cooking or keep it for collection and other purposes.

As the restaurant becomes more successful, players can upgrade their equipment, unlock more space and customise their restaurant to make it their own.

The overall goal is to start with a small restaurant where the player does most of the work, then gradually build it into a successful business run by a team of fish workers.

## 🎮 Main Gameplay Features

### 🎣 Fishing and Fish Collection

- Fish along the Eritrean coast near the Red Sea.
- Catch different fish species, with different rarities and appearances.
- Each fish can have random stats, sizes and colours.
- Fish can have different skill levels, from Level 1 to Level 3, affecting how good they are at certain jobs.
- Upgrade fishing rods to improve fishing speed and luck.
- Collect rare fish with unique colours and useful stats.

The randomised system gives players a reason to keep fishing. Even if a fish has poor worker stats, its colour or rarity could still make it valuable.

### 🐟 Hiring Fish Workers

One of the main features is being able to hire the fish you catch to work in the restaurant.

Different fish could be better at different jobs, such as cooking or other restaurant tasks. Players will need to decide which fish are worth hiring based on their stats.

At the beginning, the player will probably have to do most of the work themselves. As they collect better fish and hire more workers, the restaurant can gradually become more automated.

Players will also be able to name their fish and watch them working around the restaurant.

### 🍳 Cooking and Restaurant Management

Fish caught during fishing can be used to prepare traditional Eritrean dishes for customers.

Players will need to manage their ingredients, prepare food and keep the restaurant running. Fish workers can help with cooking and other tasks as the player progresses.

A planned feature is a fish storage tank near the beach. When the tank fills up, players will need to transport it to the kitchen area using a wheelbarrow so the fish can be used for cooking.

The restaurant starts off requiring more input from the player, but better workers and equipment should make it easier to manage over time.

### 🪑 Restaurant Customisation

Players will be able to customise their restaurant and make it feel unique.

Planned features include:

- Moving furniture and changing the restaurant layout.
- Customising colours and decorations.
- Upgrading the size of the restaurant.
- Improving the outside area.
- Expanding storage and cooking facilities.

I think this is an important part of the game because it gives players freedom to create a restaurant that suits their own style.

### ⬆️ Upgrades and Progression

Players will earn money from running the restaurant and have opportunities to earn upgrade points by serving dishes.

Upgrade points could be spent on things such as:

- Better fishing rods.
- Improved fishing speed and luck.
- Larger fish storage tanks.
- Better cooking equipment, including stoves, ovens and microwaves.
- A bigger restaurant and improved facilities.

Players will also be able to buy fish food and treats, including basic and premium options, to help their workers perform faster.

The aim is to make progression feel rewarding, with the restaurant becoming more successful as players improve their equipment and staff.

## 🌊 Cultural Inspiration

A major part of The Fish Fryer is its focus on Eritrean culture and the marine life of the Red Sea.

Instead of making a generic restaurant game, we want to include traditional Eritrean dishes and fish species associated with the region. This gives the game its own identity and could introduce players to foods and marine life they might not already know about.

The fish available in the game will influence the restaurant's cooking system, connecting the fishing and management gameplay.

I think this is important because the setting should be more than just a background. It should influence the fish players can catch, the food they prepare and the overall identity of the game.

We will need to research the fish species and traditional dishes properly to make sure they are represented accurately.

## ✨ What Makes the Game Interesting?

### 🎲 Randomised Rewards

The RNG system means players will not always know what they are going to catch. Fish can have different stats, colours, sizes and rarities.

A fish with low cooking stats might still be valuable because of its rare colour, while another fish might be much better as a worker.

This creates different reasons to keep fishing and collecting.

### 🎨 Creative Freedom

Customising the restaurant gives players control over how their business looks. They can move furniture, choose decorations and expand their space as they progress.

The aim is to make this a relaxing part of the game, rather than forcing players to follow one specific layout.

### 📈 Progression and Automation

Players begin by doing more of the restaurant work themselves. As they hire fish workers and purchase upgrades, more tasks can be handled automatically.

This creates a sense of progression because players can see their restaurant grow from a small business into a more developed operation.

### 🐠 A Unique Concept

The idea of running a restaurant staffed by fish with arms and legs gives the game a silly and memorable style.

Combining this with fishing, restaurant management and Eritrean cultural inspiration should help the game stand out from other cosy games.

## 🎯 Target Audience

Our main target audience is around **12–17 years old**, particularly players who enjoy cosy games, collecting systems, customisation and light management gameplay.

The game is intended to be relaxing, without the pressure of constant timers or intense combat.

I think the randomised fish system could appeal to this audience because collecting rare fish gives players something to work towards. Even a fish with poor stats could be worth keeping because of its appearance or rarity.

The restaurant customisation and gradual automation also give players different ways to enjoy the game, whether they prefer collecting, designing or managing their business.

The game is mainly aimed at PC because mouse controls would work well for fishing, interacting with objects, preparing food and arranging furniture.

## 🔍 Related Games and Inspiration

These games have influenced different parts of our concept. The Fish Fryer takes inspiration from their mechanics but aims to combine them into its own experience.

### 🎣 Webfishing

Webfishing is one of the closest comparisons to our fishing and collecting ideas.

It inspired the idea of catching different fish, collecting them and improving fishing equipment. We want to expand on this by giving fish random stats and colours, allowing them to have different uses outside of fishing.

### 🍳 Overcooked

Overcooked is based around preparing and serving food while managing different kitchen tasks.

Our game takes inspiration from restaurant cooking and task management, but aims to make the experience much calmer. Instead of focusing on frantic time pressure, players can gradually improve their restaurant and rely more on their fish workers.

The fishing system also connects directly to the restaurant because caught fish can become ingredients for the food being served.

### 🍕 Good Pizza, Great Pizza

Good Pizza, Great Pizza is another useful comparison because of its customer orders and restaurant management.

One idea we could take inspiration from is having customers order food that the restaurant can actually provide. For example, customers could choose from dishes that are currently available based on the fish and ingredients in stock.

### 🏪 Tycoon Games

Tycoon games have influenced our upgrade and progression systems.

Players can gradually improve their equipment, expand their restaurant and build a more efficient business. This gives players long-term goals instead of completing everything straight away.

## 🤖 Worker Patrol System

One mechanic I find interesting is the worker patrol system.

The idea is that players can control where their fish workers go and which tasks they carry out. This takes inspiration from management games such as Prison Architect, where players can organise how characters move around and perform jobs.

For example, a cooking worker might follow a sequence like this:

1. Go to the ingredient storage.
2. Collect the required ingredients.
3. Walk to the cooking station.
4. Wait until the food has finished cooking.
5. Collect the finished dish.
6. Take it to the next preparation or serving area.

We have discussed using a visual command system inspired by Scratch, where players connect commands or drag arrows to create a sequence of tasks.

This could make the worker system easier to understand and give players more control over how their restaurant operates.

It would also make the automation more interesting because players would need to organise their workers and restaurant layout effectively.
